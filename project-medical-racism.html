Medical Racism

A public-sociology video on medical racism and eugenics, created for the collaborative Spring 2026 SOC 379 course at the University of Richmond.

Watch the video on YouTube ↗

Project Focus

The video examines the history of medical racism and eugenics in Richmond and Virginia, including the 1924 Virginia Health Bulletin and Virginia’s Racial Integrity Act. Using archival sources and objects, it turns historical research into a public-sociology presentation.

Course & Learning

The project was created for a collaborative, multi-lecture SOC 379 course in Spring 2026. The interdisciplinary cohort brought together students in American Studies, Biology, Dance, Sociology, and Theatre.

The course offered me an opportunity to learn across disciplines and work with primary sources. Shaping that historical research into a video also let me practice making it accessible to a public audience.
