MLS School-Assignment Accuracy

A collaborative study of whether residential listing records accurately match verified school attendance boundaries.

Overview

The project compares public real-estate record assignments with independently verified geographic school boundaries.

Contribution

My work includes spatial validation, R workflows, geocoding, and communicating the limits of both listing data and attendance-boundary records.

Publication status

Collaborative research is in presentation and manuscript development. Property-level locations are not published on this portfolio.
